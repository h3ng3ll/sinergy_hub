## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I edit my profile?

Open the side drawer and tap **Account**. From there you can update your name, photo, and other profile details.

### How do I change my notification preferences?

Open the side drawer and tap **Notifications**. You can also adjust system-level permissions in your device settings.

### How do I delete my account?

Contact us at alexandr132135@gmail.com with the email address associated with your account, and we will process the deletion request in accordance with our Privacy Notice.

### I'm not receiving push notifications

Make sure notifications are enabled for Sinergy Hub in your device settings. If the issue persists, try logging out and back in to refresh the notification token.

## Reporting a Bug

When reporting a bug, please include:

- A short description of what you were doing
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Your device model and OS version
- The app version (shown at the bottom of the side drawer)

This helps us reproduce and fix the issue faster.
